# HypeWave

HypeWave finds the funniest and most hype GTA 6 moments from live Twitch streams and posts them as YouTube Shorts.

- YouTube: [youtube.com/@HypeWave12e](https://www.youtube.com/@HypeWave12e)
- Every clip credits and links the original streamer.
- Streamers: want a clip removed or want to be featured? [Open a request here](https://github.com/ekcna/hypewave/issues).
